> Cách dùng: dán KHỐI STYLE + KHỐI NHÂN VẬT + 1 cảnh. Veo 3 tự sinh cả tiếng động.
> Xuất 16:9, mỗi cảnh 5-6 giây. Đặt tên file masoi-1→4.mp4, ketien-1→4.mp4, don.mp4.

## KHỐI STYLE — dán kèm MỌI cảnh

pixels, limited color palette of deep navy blues and purples, warm yellow window lights,
dithered gradients, subtle CRT scanline overlay, 2D flat side-scrolling perspective like a
classic platformer cutscene, smooth 12fps pixel animation, no watermark, 16:9.

fur, two pointy triangular ears, small gray snout, big expressive white eyes, fluffy gray
tail, wearing a plain pink t-shirt and dark navy pants, barefoot with small claws, sneaky
